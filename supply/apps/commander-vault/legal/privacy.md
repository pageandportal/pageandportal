# Privacy Policy — Commander Vault

**32 Deck Challenge Companion**

**Last updated:** November 2026

Commander Vault is built to keep your deck tracking data on your device. We do not operate user accounts and we do not sell personal information.

## What stays on your device

- Commander names, bracket, build status, notes, and saved card art URLs for your 32 deck slots
- This information is stored locally using your browser or app storage (IndexedDB)
- On iPhone and iPad (coming with the iOS app), if you turn on iCloud backup (off by default), a copy of this deck data is also saved to your own iCloud account by Apple. Commander Vault cannot access it, and purchases are never included.

## What uses the internet

- Commander search and autocomplete send what you type to the Scryfall API to find cards
- Card artwork may load from Scryfall-hosted image URLs after you select a commander
- Scryfall receives these requests directly from your device and may log them under its own privacy policy
- If you add a deck link from Archidekt, ManaBox, or Moxfield, the app may contact that site to read the commander from the public deck
- If you tap View on EDHREC, the EDHREC website opens with your commander's name in the web address
- On Android, the app asks Google Play whether a newer version is available so it can offer an in-app update
- No deck list content is uploaded to Commander Vault servers (this app has no backend)

## Purchases

- Commander Vault offers an optional one-time purchase to unlock the full 32-slot challenge
- Purchases are processed by Google Play on Android and by Apple's App Store on iPhone and iPad, under their own terms and privacy policies
- We do not receive or store your payment card details
- The app receives purchase status from the store only to unlock and restore the purchase on the same store account
- Unlock status is never included in backups

## What we do not collect

- No analytics SDKs are included in this app
- No advertising identifiers
- No email or account registration inside the app

## Children

This app is not directed at children under 13.
