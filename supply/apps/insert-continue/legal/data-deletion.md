# Request data deletion — Insert Continue

**Arcade Mini Games**

Insert Continue (Page & Portal / James Archibald) does not create user accounts. Use the steps below to remove what you can on the device, and to ask us about anything we hold.

## How to request deletion

1. **Email** [pageandportal@gmail.com](mailto:pageandportal@gmail.com) with the subject **Insert Continue — data deletion**.
2. Tell us what you want removed (for example an email you sent us earlier).
3. We will reply and action what we hold, or point you to the right Google tools for data held by Google’s services.

## What is deleted or kept

| Data | What happens |
|------|----------------|
| Local high scores, initials, and settings on your phone | Cleared if you **clear the app’s storage** or **uninstall** the app. We never receive this data. |
| Email you send us (support / privacy) | We keep it only to reply. Email us to ask us to delete that correspondence. |
| Advertising, analytics, and crash data processed by Google (AdMob, Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics) | Held by Google under their policies. Use Google’s published privacy / ad / account tools, or email us and we will help you find the right place. Turning **Share usage data** off in Settings stops the app sending new Analytics and Crashlytics data. |
| Purchases | Handled by Google Play. Refunds and purchase history follow Google Play’s processes; we only see unlock status. |

## Retention

We do not run our own game servers. How long Google keeps ads, analytics, and crash data is set by Google’s product policies. Local data stays on the device until you clear it or uninstall.
