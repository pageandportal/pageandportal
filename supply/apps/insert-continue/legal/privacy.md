# Privacy Policy — Insert Continue

**Arcade Mini Games**

**Last updated:** 24 November 2026

**Contact:** [pageandportal@gmail.com](mailto:pageandportal@gmail.com)

Insert Continue is a pocket arcade of original mini games. It is made by Page & Portal, run by James Archibald in Nelson, New Zealand. It does not create user accounts and it does not sell your personal information.

This policy describes what stays on your phone and what Google’s services may process when you play. It is written for the shipping Android build.

## What stays on your device

- Local high-score tables and three-letter initials
- Settings (for example screen colour, CRT on or off, mute, and whether you share usage data)
- Purchase unlock status for optional in-app items, when those items are available, as reported by Google Play

None of this is uploaded to a Page & Portal server. Insert Continue has no game backend of its own.

## What uses the internet

When the device is online, the app may contact Google services for:

- **Advertising** — Google AdMob shows ads between games (never in the middle of a round). In some games you may choose to watch a short rewarded ad to continue a run, once per run.
- **Analytics** — Google Firebase Analytics receives usage events (for example which game you started, how a run ended, and whether an ad was shown or failed). These are linked to a random app identifier, not your name or email. Session information may also be recorded by Firebase.
- **Crash reports** — Google Firebase Crashlytics receives crash and diagnostic information so problems can be fixed. These reports are also linked to a random app identifier, not your name or email.
- **Purchases** — Google Play Billing handles any optional one-time purchases (for example Remove ads, when that purchase is offered). The app only receives unlock status from Play.

Google may process device or advertising identifiers, approximate location inferred from IP address, app activity, and diagnostic data under Google’s own policies. Google may process this information outside New Zealand, including in the United States.

Where consent is required (for example the EEA and UK), ads are personalised only if you agree. Elsewhere, Google may personalise ads unless you limit or reset your advertising ID in your device settings.

## Advertising and consent

Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and UK), Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) asks for consent before personalised ads. You can reopen privacy / consent options from Settings where that control is available.

You can also use your device and Google account advertising controls (for example resetting or limiting the advertising ID).

## Usage data switch

Settings includes **Share usage data** (on by default). When this is off, the app stops sending Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics data. Advertising still follows the consent rules above. Events already queued on the device may send when you next go online if usage data was on when they were recorded.

## What we do not collect

- No accounts, names, or email addresses inside the app
- No precise location, contacts, photos, or microphone access for gameplay
- No payment card details — Google Play processes payments; we never see your card number
- No free-text messages to our servers

High scores and initials stay on the phone unless you clear the app’s data or uninstall.

## Your rights

You can ask us to see or correct personal information we hold about you. The only personal information we hold is what you send us by email, which we keep only to reply. For data held by Google’s services (ads, analytics, crash reports, purchases), use Google’s published tools and contacts, or email us and we’ll help you find the right place.

## Children

Insert Continue is not directed at children under 13. The Google Play target age band is 13+. If you think a child under 13 has used the app, contact us.

## Data retention and deletion

How long Google keeps advertising, analytics, and crash data is governed by Google’s policies for AdMob, Firebase Analytics, and Crashlytics. Clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling removes local scores and settings on that device.
