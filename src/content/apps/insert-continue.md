features:
  - title: In the cabinet
    description: >-
      Original games, built from scratch. Paddle & Pixel — a side-on paddle duel
      against the computer; first to seven wins. Neon Snake — eat, grow, and don’t
      bite your own tail; walls or wrap-around. Brick Buster — ten hand-built brick
      layouts, three lives, one ball, no power-up circus. Formation Fire — hold the
      line against eight waves of descending drones. Lane Hopper — get the rabbits
      across roads, railway tracks, a sinking marsh and a river before the clock
      runs out. Shattered Earth — a
      turn-based artillery duel: set the angle and power, blast the hills, outlast
      the other tank.
  - title: Short rounds
    description: >-
      Built for a bus stop or a coffee break. Simple touch controls, each game on
      the screen orientation that suits it.
  - title: Plays offline
    description: No account and no sign-in. A round does not need a connection.
  - title: A green screen you can switch off
    description: >-
      Classic phosphor green, with a plain white screen if you prefer. Subtle
      scanlines and a light vignette, and a mute button that means it.
  - title: High scores stay on the phone
    description: Cabinet-style local tables. Three-letter initials. Your scores are never uploaded.
  - title: Fair play
    description: >-
      Free to play, with ads only between games — never in the middle of a round.
      In some games you can choose to watch a short ad to continue a run, once per
      run. A one-off way to turn ads off comes after launch. This is not an emulator
      and not a set of licensed remakes.
