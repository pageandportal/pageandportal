# Privacy Policy — Portal Pages: Times Tables

**Effective date:** 23 May 2026  
**Last updated:** 23 May 2026

This policy describes how **Portal Pages: Times Tables** (the “App”) handles information when you install and use it on an Android device. The App is published on Google Play by **Page and Portal** (“we”, “us”, “our”).

**Contact:** [pageandportal@gmail.com](mailto:pageandportal@gmail.com)  
**Website:** [https://pageandportal.github.io/pageandportal/apps/portal-pages-times-tables/privacy](https://pageandportal.github.io/pageandportal/apps/portal-pages-times-tables/privacy)

---

## Summary

- **No account** is required.
- **No ads** and **no in-app purchases** in the current version.
- **No analytics or advertising SDKs** — we do not receive usage or behaviour data from the App.
- **No internet connection** is required for normal use after installation.
- **Practice progress and settings stay on your device** unless you use Android’s backup features or move data by changing devices yourself.

---

## 1. Information we collect

We **do not** ask for a name, email address, date of birth, phone number, or other information that directly identifies a child or adult.

We **do not** operate servers that receive personal data from the App. The App does not include third-party analytics, crash-reporting, or advertising tools that send data to us or to ad networks.

When you contact us by email (for example about support), we receive only what you choose to send in that message.

---

## 2. Information stored on your device

To provide practice, progress, and settings, the App stores data **locally** on the device:

| Type of data | Purpose | Examples |
|--------------|---------|----------|
| **Practice history** | Show progress, mastery, and parent summaries | Which multiplication facts were attempted, whether answers were correct, timestamps |
| **App settings** | Remember your choices | Sound and music toggles, session length, accessibility options, optional practice modes |
| **Optional parent PIN** | Restrict access to grown-up settings | A **hashed** PIN (not stored in plain text) |
| **Welcome / onboarding state** | Avoid repeating first-run steps | Whether the welcome flow was completed |
| **Last practised tables** | Convenience on the home screen | Table numbers recently used |

This information is **not** linked to an online account in the App and is **not** transmitted to us by the App during ordinary use.

---

## 3. Device permissions

The App may request:

| Permission | Why |
|------------|-----|
| **Notifications** (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`) | Only if a grown-up turns on **practice reminders** in settings. Reminders are **off by default**. You can deny or revoke this permission in Android settings at any time. |

The App does **not** request location, camera, microphone, contacts, or internet access for its core features. Learn mode may use the device’s **built-in text-to-speech** engine to read facts aloud; speech is processed on the device by Android, not sent to us.

---

## 4. Home screen widget

If you add the optional **practice widget**, Android may show suggested tables based on data already stored on the device. The widget does not send that data to us.

---

## 5. Children and families

The App is designed for children practising times tables, with optional grown-up controls (such as a PIN for settings). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the App.

Because we do not collect personal data through the App, we do not sell or share children’s personal information for advertising, and the App does not show ads in the current version.

Parents and guardians can review progress in the App, use optional PIN protection for settings, and **uninstall the App** or **clear app data** in Android settings to remove locally stored information.

---

## 6. Sharing of information

We **do not sell** your information.

We **do not share** App usage or practice data with third parties for marketing or analytics.

Limited sharing may occur only in these situations:

- **You choose to contact us** (e.g. support email).
- **Google Play** — Google processes information according to its own policies when you download, update, or interact with the store (see [Google Play Terms](https://play.google.com/about/play-terms/) and [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)).
- **Legal requirements** — if we are required to disclose information by applicable law or a valid legal process.

---

## 7. Android backup and new devices

The App may allow **Android backup** (for example to a Google account), depending on your device and system settings. Backup content is controlled by Google and your device manufacturer, not by us. If you restore the App on another device, practice data and settings may be restored with it.

To remove App data from a device: **Settings → Apps → Portal Pages: Times Tables → Storage → Clear data** (wording may vary by device).

---

## 8. Data retention

Data remains on your device until you clear app data, uninstall the App, or replace the device without restoring a backup. We do not retain copies of your practice history on our servers because the App does not send it to us.

---

## 9. Security

We design the App to keep information on the device. Optional parent settings use a **hashed PIN** rather than storing the PIN in readable form. No method of storage is perfectly secure; please use device lock screens and PIN features appropriate for your family.

---

## 10. International users

If you are in the United Kingdom or European Economic Area, you may have rights under data protection law (such as access, correction, or deletion) in relation to personal data **we** hold — for example, in support emails. Because the App does not collect personal data through its operation, most requests will concern information you sent us directly or data on your device that you control via Android.

Our lawful basis for processing any contact information you send us is typically **legitimate interests** (responding to enquiries) or **consent**, depending on the context.

---

## 11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App or legal requirements change. We will post the new version on this page and change the “Last updated” date. For significant changes, we may also note them in the App or store listing where appropriate.
